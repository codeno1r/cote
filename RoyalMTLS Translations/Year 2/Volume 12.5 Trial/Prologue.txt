Prologue - Behind the Scenes

THE TENSE END-OF-YEAR SPECIAL EXAM period continued.

Cameras were set up from multiple viewing angles.

Discussions between the participants unfolded on the monitor.

A one-on-one battle between representatives, where no one was allowed to intervene.

The full picture, with its distorted rules, wasn’t even disclosed to the homeroom teachers until immediately beforehand.

Despite being suddenly thrust onto the battlefield, the students earnestly faced the challenge head-on.

Hoshinomiya Chie watched over the special exam with bated breath while suppressing her frustrating feelings.

She desired only one thing: the victory of her class.

Her jaw hurt from clenching her teeth so hard, but she had yet to even notice it.

She intended to use any means necessary in this special exam if she was able to.

The rules of the end-of-year special exam, as initially informed, originally allowed the teachers to have some limited room to get involved.

However, once the lid on the actual rules was opened, it turned out that a completely different exam was being implemented.

The reason for the sudden change wasn’t explained by the school and remained unclear.

It was a mystery, not only to Hoshinomiya, but also to all the teachers involved with the second-year students.

However, now the reason was clear.

An anomaly, no, a spectator, sat before her.

The presence of this visitor had thrown a wrench in her plans. 

"Why..."

Hoshinomiya muttered in a low voice.

The incident occurred just when hope was beginning to show itself—after Horikita Suzune’s defeat.

Ayanokōji Kiyotaka appeared resolutely, with words, conduct, and merciless strategies that wouldn't make one think that he was a student.

Ichinose Honami was hanging her head, confirming her defeat.

"It's unfair, something like this..."

Though no one heard her, she couldn't help but lament.

The overwhelming presence of a joker.

The strongest hand that Chabashira held made her painfully aware that there was no chance of winning.

All she could do was watch through the screen, and the outcome was mercilessly decided.

"The end-of-year special exam has now concluded. Thank you for your hard work."

The one in charge of the school, Chairman Sakayanagi, spoke with an appreciative and inquiring tone.

After watching the special exam to its end along with the four homeroom teachers and several men in black suits, the visiting man slowly rose from his elegant chair.

Seeing this, the men in black, standing by behind him, hurried to prepare for their departure.

"Thank you for your help this time, Chairman Sakayanagi. I appreciate the thorough preparations you've made."

Coffee was poured into an undeniably expensive-looking cup alongside the most beautiful tea sweets one had ever seen.

Although they had gone almost untouched, a hand was extended to Sakayanagi as a sign of gratitude.

Sakayanagi hurriedly took his hand and bowed deeply.

"It's nothing, Prime Minister Kijima. Thank you very much for taking the trouble to come here."

It was the current prime minister, Kijima.

A man who was also a proponent of the Advanced Nurturing High School had come just to observe this special exam.

Since this was the case, Hoshinomiya had no choice but to be content with the situation.

With the prime minister visiting, it wouldn’t be surprising if some changes were made.

Kijima turned his gaze back to the monitor.

On the screen—ignoring Ichinose, who was standing in a daze, unable to support herself—was the figure of Ayanokōji leaving the room.

"It's truly regrettable that there were expulsions, but the notion that each student has the freedom to utilize the exam's rules should be respected."

"It helps our school greatly to hear you say that. Minister Ogiso has strongly requested to operate without fear of the risk of expulsion—"

Sakayanagi began to explain the expulsions, but Kijima gently stopped him.

Ogiso refers to the Minister of Education, Culture, Sports, Science, and Technology, which the teachers understood while they exchanged glances.

"I understand. But of course, we can ensure that the student who ended up being expelled will receive thorough support, correct?"

"Naturally. We will promptly contact several schools for her transfer that match her academic abilities and personality."

"Is that so? Please take care of it well."

Kijima checked the time on his modest-looking wristwatch and turned his back to leave the room.

Sakayanagi hurriedly followed him.

"As planned, we will visit again on April 1st, so please take care of us well."

"Of course. Company President Kōenji was also looking forward to meeting with you, Prime Minister Kijima."

"That's good to hear. Since it’s been three years, I'm looking forward to meeting him as well."

While having this conversation, Sakayanagi and Kijima left the room.

The four homeroom teachers left behind sighed in relief from the heavy atmosphere.

"Congratulations, Sae-chan. Even if it's temporary, you're in Class A now."

"I'd like to thank you, but the exam just ended... and there's also the matter of Maezono who got expelled. I can't just happily celebrate."

Is the expulsion of a single student more significant than the joy of being promoted to Class A?

Hoshinomiya felt irritation growing inside her as Chabashira answered with a serious expression.

In reality, she must be mocking my continual failures, she thought.

Or perhaps... she doesn't even consider me worth noticing.

With each surge of frustrating emotions, her resentment grew deeper.

I will never let this woman win.

I won't let her graduate from Class A.

Such a resolve, akin to murderous intent, swelled up far more than before the exam.
